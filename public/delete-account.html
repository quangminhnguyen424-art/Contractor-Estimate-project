Delete your account

Xóa tài khoản trong app: Tôi → tài khoản (email của bạn ở trên cùng) → Xóa tài khoản. Không mở được app? Gửi email từ địa chỉ đăng nhập tới dream@upgradeshome.com với tiêu đề "Delete my account". Mọi dữ liệu trên cloud bị xóa vĩnh viễn; bản sao lưu bị xóa trong 30 ngày.

In the app (fastest)

Open Studwise AI, go to Me → your account (your email at the top) → Delete my account, and confirm. The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email dream@upgradeshome.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm it is you and delete the account within 7 days.

Ask to delete my account

What is deleted

Your sign-in, your saved work (estimates, quotes, contracts, invoices, job sites, schedules, prices, notes), photos and receipts, client signing records, ADU leads, support history and AI usage counts. Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days.

What stays: documents you already sent to clients (they keep their copies), and records we must keep by law, such as payment records for paid plans.

Work saved only on your phone stays on that phone until you delete the app. Export a copy first if you want one: Me → Backup.
