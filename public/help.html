Bidwell AI Help Center

How-to guides for contractors. In the app, tap the ? on Home to chat with Bidwell AI.

Start a new estimate Tạo báo giá mới

Tap the orange + at the bottom. Pick the type of work, then talk, type notes, add photos or scan the rooms. Tap Continue, answer the few questions (anything you skip uses a standard assumption) and tap Build estimate. Your prices, markup and tax are used automatically.

Measure a roof from satellite Đo mái nhà bằng vệ tinh

Start a new estimate and choose Roof replacement. On the questions screen, enter the job address and tap Measure roof. You get the roof area, squares, pitch of every plane, roof shape, height, edge lengths and waste. Tap Use these numbers to fill the estimate. Always confirm on site before ordering materials.

Scan rooms in 3D Quét phòng 3D

On an iPhone with LiDAR (Pro models), tap Scan 3D on Home and walk the room slowly. The app draws the floor plan and fills in floor and wall areas. Without LiDAR you can type the room sizes on the Floor plan screen.

Edit the client quote before sending Sửa báo giá trước khi gửi

Open the estimate, tap Client quote, then Edit. You can change headings, what is included, the scope summary, Not included, notes and how long the quote is valid, and choose a price per section or one total. Tap Help me write and Bidwell drafts the wording for you. To change the price itself, change the markup or Your items on the estimate.

Get the quote signed Cho khách ký báo giá

Open the estimate. Tap Sign to let the client sign on your phone, or Send a link to sign online so they sign on their own phone (you must be logged in). When they sign, the estimate is marked Won and you get a notice.

Add a change order Thêm phát sinh (change order)

Open the estimate, go to Contract & payments, and tap Add change order. Describe the work and price; the client approves it by signing on your phone or from a link. Approved change orders are added to the contract price.

Invoices and payments Hoá đơn và thanh toán

Open the estimate and go to Contract & payments. Create invoices from the payment schedule, record payments (cash, check, Zelle, card) and see what is still owed. Home shows the total to collect and anything overdue.

Automatic follow-ups Nhắc khách tự động

Open a sent estimate and tap Follow-ups. First record that the client agreed to be contacted (required by law), pick text or email, then set the days. Bidwell reminds you on those days between 8am and 8pm and stops when the client says no or the job is won or lost.

Job site photos, before and after Ảnh công trình, trước và sau

Open a job site and tap the big camera button at the bottom. Pick the area and Before, Progress or After, then take the photos. Each area has its own tasks your crew can mark Done or Needs review.

Send the client a photo update Gửi ảnh cập nhật cho khách

Open the job site and tap Send the client a photo update. Bidwell picks the newest photos and writes the message. Prices, costs and receipts are never included.

Crew, subs and work orders Thợ, thầu phụ và phiếu việc

Add your people in Settings > Crew & subs with their rate and language (English, Tiếng Việt, Español). On a job site, Crew work order makes a task list in their language with no prices. Each morning Home asks if your crew showed up; Worked adds the labor cost to the job.

Job money and receipts (private) Tiền công trình và hoá đơn mua (riêng tư)

Open a job site and tap Money. Snap a receipt and Bidwell reads it, adds it to the job's costs, checks it off the buy list and learns your real prices. Receipts and profit are private: never on client or crew documents.

Schedule a visit or crew day Lên lịch

Tap Schedule at the bottom, then +. Choose the kind (site visit, crew day, inspection…), the job, time and reminder, and pick the crew for crew days.

Change my prices, markup and tax Đổi giá, markup và thuế

Go to Settings > My pricing for your price per square foot by finish level, markup, minimum markup and sales tax. The Price Book lets you change any labor rate or material price; every new estimate uses them.

Account, backup and a new phone Tài khoản, sao lưu và đổi điện thoại

Your work is saved to your account automatically. On a new phone, install Bidwell and log in: your estimates, jobs and photos come back. Settings > Account shows when it last synced.

Forgot password Quên mật khẩu

On the Log in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email and open the link we send you. Choose a new password there, then log in again in the app. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive in a few minutes.

Delete my account and data Xoá tài khoản và dữ liệu

Settings > Account > Delete my account removes your account, your cloud data and all backed-up photos for good. Settings > Erase removes the data on this phone.

How the AI works (and its limits) AI hoạt động thế nào

Bidwell's AI drafts estimates, quote wording and messages from your notes and photos, using your own prices. It can be wrong: always check quantities and prices before sending. Each account has a daily AI limit that resets every day.

Quotes in Vietnamese Báo giá tiếng Việt

On the Client quote screen choose English or Tiếng Việt; each language can be edited separately. Crew work orders also come in Español.

QuickBooks, Google Sheets, Calendar Kết nối QuickBooks, Sheets, Lịch

Settings > Connected apps sends won jobs, invoices and payments to your other tools through Zapier, Make or a webhook, and can export to a spreadsheet.
