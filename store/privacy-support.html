Bidwell AI: privacy and support

Bidwell AI is an estimating app for construction contractors. This page explains what happens to your information and how to get help.

Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version

  • Your estimates, prices, clients and photos are stored on your own phone. There is no Bidwell AI account and we do not run a database of your data.
  • When you use the AI features, the text and photos you send are processed by Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to produce the answer.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics, and we never sell your information.

What stays on your device

Your company profile, prices, past jobs, estimates, client names and addresses, job notes, photos you attach, floor plans, chat history and your Claude API key are saved only on your device. Backups are files you export and keep yourself; we do not receive them.

What is sent to Anthropic, and when

Only when you use an AI feature, the app sends the following to Anthropic’s Claude service to produce the result:

FeatureWhat is sent
Reading a jobYour job notes, the photos you chose (resized), the questions for that type of work, and your setup answers and price history for that type
Assistant chatYour recent messages, plus your company name, prices, setup answers and a short list of recent estimates (title, type, status, price) so answers fit your business
Reading an old quoteThe quote text you pasted

Anthropic processes this under its own terms and privacy policy. Requests sent with your own API key are covered by your agreement with Anthropic.

Voice, camera and room scans

  • Voice: speech is turned into text by your phone’s built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google, under their privacy policies). The app keeps only the text.
  • Camera and photos: used only for the photos you add to a job.
  • LiDAR room scans: processed on your device by Apple RoomPlan. Only the measurements are saved with the estimate.

Your choices

  • Use the app without AI features: nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Remove a finding Claude made, or edit any answer, before you build an estimate.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Erase all data on this device, or delete the app.

Children

Bidwell AI is a business tool for contractors and is not directed to children under 13.

Common questions

How do I turn on Claude AI?

Create an API key at console.anthropic.com (API Keys), add credit and set a monthly spending limit there. In Bidwell AI, open Settings → Claude AI, paste the key and tap Save and test key.

Which phones can scan rooms?

Room scanning uses LiDAR, found on iPhone Pro and Pro Max models (12 Pro and later) and iPad Pro. On other phones, measure rooms by hand on the Floor plan screen; the plan and measurements work the same way.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings → Export backup and save the file. On the new phone, choose Restore a backup during setup, or Settings → Restore from backup.

The microphone button doesn't work.

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Bidwell AI in your phone’s Settings. You can always tap the box and use the microphone on your keyboard instead.

Are the prices the AI suggests final?

No. Estimates are built from your own prices and answers, and Claude’s suggestions are marked for you to check. Always review an estimate before sending it to a client.
