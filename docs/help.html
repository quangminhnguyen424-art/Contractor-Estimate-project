Bidwell AI Help Center

How-to guides for contractors. In the app, tap the ? on Home to chat with Bidwell AI.

Start a new estimate Tạo báo giá mới

Tap the orange + at the bottom. Describe the job by typing or tapping the microphone; tap the + in the message box to add photos or scan the rooms. Tap the arrow, check the details Bidwell filled in (anything you skip uses a standard assumption) and tap Build estimate. The job opens with the price and one button: Review and send quote. For repeat work, choose Quick price: the size, your price per unit and how many.

Measure a roof from satellite Đo mái nhà bằng vệ tinh

Tap the orange +, choose Roof replacement under Type of work and tap the arrow. On Check the details, enter the job address and tap Measure roof. You get the roof area, squares, pitch of every plane, roof shape, height, edge lengths and waste. Tap Use these numbers to fill the estimate. Always confirm on site before ordering materials.

Scan rooms in 3D Quét phòng 3D

On an iPhone with LiDAR (Pro models), start a new estimate, tap the + in the message box and choose Scan rooms in 3D, then walk the room slowly. The app draws the floor plan and fills in floor and wall areas. Without LiDAR you can type the room sizes on the Floor plan screen.

Edit the client quote before sending Sửa báo giá trước khi gửi

Open the job in the Jobs tab and tap Client quote, then Edit. You can change headings, what is included, the scope summary, Not included, notes and how long the quote is valid, and choose a price per section or one total. Tap Help me write and Bidwell drafts the wording for you. To change the price itself, open Price details on the job and change the markup, or tap ⋯ > Edit job details. To fix the job's name, the client or the address, tap them at the top of the job.

Get the quote signed Cho khách ký báo giá

Open the job in the Jobs tab. Tap Review and send quote, then Send > Text a link to sign: the client signs on their own phone (you must be logged in). Or tap Sign on the quote and hand them your phone; once the quote is sent, the job itself shows Sign on this phone. When they sign, the job moves to Signed and you get a notice.

Add a change order Thêm phát sinh (change order)

On a signed or won job, tap Contract and payments, then + New change order. Describe the work and price; the client approves it by signing on your phone or from a link. Approved change orders are added to the contract price.

Invoices and payments Hoá đơn và thanh toán

Contract and payments appears on the job once the client has signed or you mark the job won (also under the job's Money view). Tap Invoice beside a payment step to make its invoice, record payments (cash, check, Zelle, card) and see what is still owed. Home shows the total to collect and anything overdue.

Automatic follow-ups Nhắc khách tự động

Open a sent quote in the Jobs tab and tap Follow-ups. First record that the client agreed to be contacted (required by law), pick text or email, then set the days. Bidwell reminds you on those days between 8am and 8pm and stops when the client says no or the job is won or lost.

Job site photos, before and after Ảnh công trình, trước và sau

Open the job (Jobs tab), tap Job site, then Take photos. Pick the area and Before, Progress or After, then take the photos. Each area has its own to-dos your crew can mark Done or Needs review.

Send the client a photo update Gửi ảnh cập nhật cho khách

Open the job, tap Job site, then Photo update for the client. Bidwell picks the newest photos and writes the message. Prices, costs and receipts are never included.

Crew, subs and work orders Thợ, thầu phụ và phiếu việc

Add your people in Me > Crew and subs with their rate and language (English, Tiếng Việt, Español). On a job, Job site > Work order for the crew makes a to-do list in their language with no prices. Each morning Home asks if your crew showed up; Worked adds the labor cost to the job.

Job money and receipts (private) Tiền công trình và hoá đơn mua (riêng tư)

Open the job and tap Money. You see what the client pays, what is paid and owed, and your cost so far. Under Costs and receipts, snap a receipt and Bidwell reads it, adds it to the job's costs, checks it off the buy list and learns your real prices. Costs and profit are private: never on client or crew documents.

Schedule a visit or crew day Lên lịch

Tap Schedule at the bottom, pick the day, then tap the Add button for that day. Choose the kind (site visit, crew day, inspection…), the job, time and reminder, and pick the crew for crew days. You can also add a visit from a job's Schedule row.

Change my prices, markup and tax Đổi giá, markup và thuế

Go to Me > My pricing for your price per square foot by finish level, your past jobs and the Price Book (any labor rate or material price). Markup, minimum markup, sales tax and how long quotes are valid are under Me > Markup, tax and quote defaults. Every new estimate uses them.

Account, backup and a new phone Tài khoản, sao lưu và đổi điện thoại

Your work is saved to your account automatically. On a new phone, install Bidwell and log in: your estimates, jobs and photos come back. On the Me tab, tap the row with your email to see when it last synced, back up photos, change your password or log out.

Forgot password Quên mật khẩu

On the Log in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email and open the link we send you. Choose a new password there, then log in again in the app. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive in a few minutes.

Delete my account and data Xoá tài khoản và dữ liệu

On the Me tab, tap the row with your email, then Delete my account. It removes your account, your jobs, clients, prices and all backed-up photos for good, from the cloud and from this phone. It cannot be undone.

How the AI works (and its limits) AI hoạt động thế nào

Bidwell's AI drafts estimates, quote wording and messages from your notes and photos, using your own prices. It can be wrong: always check quantities and prices before sending. Turn it on or off under Me > Bidwell AI > Terms, privacy and AI sharing. Each account has a daily AI limit that resets every day.

Language Ngôn ngữ

The whole app, Bidwell’s answers and the voice follow one language: change it under Me > Language. What the client receives follows “Client’s language” on that job’s quote screen (English or Tiếng Việt), so you can work in your language and still send an English quote. Crew work orders go out in each person’s language.

QuickBooks, Google Sheets, Calendar Kết nối QuickBooks, Sheets, Lịch

Me > Connected apps sends won jobs, invoices and payments to your other tools through Zapier, Make or a webhook, and can export to a spreadsheet.
